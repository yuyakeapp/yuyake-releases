# Changelog

Release notes for every published Yuyake build. Each entry matches a [GitHub Release](https://github.com/yuyakeapp/yuyake-releases/releases).

## [0.0.1](https://github.com/yuyakeapp/yuyake-releases/releases/tag/v0.0.1) — 2026-10-10

### Added

- Live video wallpaper on your desktop and, optionally, your lock screen
- A built-in Discover library, plus your own clips and photo slideshows
- Schedules, Auto, rotation and collections, with no limits
- Quitting Yuyake leaves your macOS wallpaper as it was, and Settings has a Restore macOS wallpaper button
- Help menu: Send Feedback and Copy Diagnostics, sent only if you choose
- Privacy text that lists every connection Yuyake makes
