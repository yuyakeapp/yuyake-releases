# Changelog

Release notes for every published Yuyake build. Each entry matches a [GitHub Release](https://github.com/yuyakeapp/yuyake-releases/releases).

## [0.0.2](https://github.com/yuyakeapp/yuyake-releases/releases/tag/v0.0.2) — 2026-10-12

### Changed

- The last setup screen now says Yuyake starts when you log in, and that Settings can turn that off

### Fixed

- Removing Yuyake's lock screen copies, and tidying the wallpaper catalog, now leave alone any custom wallpaper entry that is not Yuyake's
- Weather and sunrise lookups send your location rounded to about a kilometre, as the privacy text says
- Copy Diagnostics also hides media file names that were logged without quotes
- Downloads refuse redirects to non-https or local-network addresses, and the Discover server can no longer point Yuyake at them

### Download

[Yuyake.dmg](https://github.com/yuyakeapp/yuyake-releases/releases/download/v0.0.2/Yuyake.dmg)

## [0.0.1](https://github.com/yuyakeapp/yuyake-releases/releases/tag/v0.0.1) — 2026-10-10

### Added

- Live video wallpaper on your desktop and, optionally, your lock screen
- A built-in Discover library, plus your own clips and photo slideshows
- Schedules, Auto, rotation and collections, with no limits
- Quitting Yuyake leaves your macOS wallpaper as it was, and Settings has a Restore macOS wallpaper button
- Help menu: Send Feedback and Copy Diagnostics, sent only if you choose
- Privacy text that lists every connection Yuyake makes

[Yuyake.dmg](https://github.com/yuyakeapp/yuyake-releases/releases/download/v0.0.1/Yuyake.dmg)
